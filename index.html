About QUANDRO

The Sound
of Now

QUANDRO is a Cape Town-based DJ and producer whose sound lives at the intersection of Afro House, Deep House and Driving, Hypnotic Deep House. Known for his meticulous track selection and rare ability to read a crowd, QUANDRO has built a reputation as one of the most compelling selectors on the South African electronic music scene.

From underground warehouse parties to upscale club residencies, QUANDRO delivers sets that are equal parts journey and catharsis — a seamless blend of classic House influences and cutting-edge contemporary production. His original productions carry the same DNA: warm basslines, intricate percussion, and an emotional depth that keeps dance floors moving from first drop to last track.

Whether headlining or supporting, in the studio or behind the decks, QUANDRO brings an uncompromising commitment to the craft of music. Every set is a conversation between artist and audience — and the dialogue is always worth having.
